Connect your Tesla

Tesla's browser blocks home-network addresses like 192.168.x.x, so it can't open this computer over normal Wi-Fi. Let the car join a hotspot from this computer instead:

  1. Connect your iPhone with a USB cable and turn on its Personal Hotspot, so the Mac gets internet over USB. (Ethernet works too.)
  2. Click Prepare car hotspot below and enter your Mac password. This changes the hotspot to an address range the car accepts.
  3. In System Settings > General > Sharing > Internet Sharing: share from iPhone USB to Wi-Fi, set a network name and password, then turn it on. If it was already on, turn it off and on again.
  4. On the car, connect to that Wi-Fi network, then open the car address that appears above.

  2. In Settings > Network & internet > Mobile hotspot, set a network name and password, then turn it on. If it was already on, turn it off and on again.
  3. On the car, connect to that Wi-Fi network, then open the car address that appears above.

Display to stream

On Windows, add a virtual monitor with a virtual display driver (see native/windows/README.md), then set its Scale to 125–150% in Display settings for readable text.
