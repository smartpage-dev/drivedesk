How it works

  • Mac or Windows: your computer becomes an extra screen in the car.
  • Android: mirror your phone to the car, with touch control. No laptop needed.
  • Nintendo Switch (custom firmware with SysDVR): stream games to the car through the Android app.
  • iPhone: through a Mac using iPhone Mirroring or AirPlay.
